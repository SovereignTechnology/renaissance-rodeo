Location

Renaissance Rodeo is on the shore of the lake in Ilopango, at its west end, a short boat ride across the water from Apulo. On Friday 13 November 2026 the gates open at 12 PM, and the prizes and grand finale are at 10 PM: see the full schedule.

Getting there

Park in Apulo, cross by boat or ferry

Coming by car or taxi? Park in Apulo, about 30 minutes from San Salvador, where there is plenty of parking, and take the 5-minute boat ride straight to the rodeo. It's faster and easier than parking at the venue, and it keeps the lakeside roads clear for everyone.

Many independent boat owners offer rides at Apulo: show them the rodeo's location on your phone and bargain for a price before you set off.

Bitcoin Life's ferry leaves from Apulo too: park your car and take the ferry straight to the rodeo, or ride Bitcoin Life's bus from San Salvador and then the ferry. Seats are limited: first come, first served. Reserve with Bitcoin Life.

Find Apulo in Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze.

Helicopter from San Salvador

Bitcoin Life flies a helicopter shuttle from San Salvador to the rodeo. At the rodeo it also runs:

  • Helicopter tours around the lake
  • One-hour ferry tours of the lake
  • Jet skis

Seats are limited: first come, first served. Reserve with Bitcoin Life · @TheBitcoinLife_.

Coming from Bitcoin Histórico

Bitcoin Histórico is the two days before, 11–12 November in Centro Histórico, San Salvador, and its ticket already includes Renaissance Rodeo. From San Salvador, fly in by helicopter, or take Bitcoin Life's bus and then the ferry from Apulo.

Before you go

  • Your ticket is in your email: have its QR code ready at the gate.
  • Locals with a DUI: show it at the gate. No ticket needed.
  • Bitcoin Histórico ticket holders: your ticket already includes Renaissance Rodeo.
  • Tickets are Bitcoin only. Visit the Bitcoin Center by The Bitcoin Hardware Store, and bring anyone who is new to bitcoin.
