About the event

El Salvador is in the midst of a Renaissance, this event is designed to promote the cattle and agricultural part of this rebirth.

By celebrating the real men and women behind the cowboy culture that has survived El Salvador’s trials, we aim for the event to be a hub where local and internationals can meet and enjoy, and develop relationships that can bolster the industry and allow it to thrive as the country surges towards becoming one of the best countries on the planet.

It will be a full afternoon of rodeo entertainment, including bull riding, mutton busting, animal fairs, barrel racing, music and barbecue.
